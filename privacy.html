Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

homelab rclone is a private application used only by its owner to keep encrypted backups of the owner's home lab in the owner's own Google Drive.

Data the application accesses

  • Only files in Google Drive that the application has created itself (scope drive.file). It cannot see or change any other files in the account.
  • The backup files are encrypted on the owner's own server before upload; Google Drive stores only encrypted content and encrypted file names.

What the application does not do

  • It is not offered to other people and does not collect data from any other user.
  • It does not share, sell or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not use Google user data for advertising, analytics or any purpose other than storing and restoring the owner's backups.

Retention and deletion

Backups are kept and deleted by the owner's backup schedule. Revoking the application's access in the Google account (myaccount.google.com/permissions) stops all access immediately.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
